Turn: drag in a circle on the knob, or scroll over it (trackpad, mouse wheel).
Push: click the cap. It stays down while its context is open; click again to close it.
Computer keyboard: Q/A W/S E/D turn the left knobs, R/F T/G Y/H the right ones, 1 to 6 push them, Shift holds SHIFT (the SHIFT button latches: click to engage, click again to release), Space starts (then: hold = fill, tap = fill on the next bar), Esc stops, Enter arms REC.
Curves: on a lane, Value adds or sets the point of the step, Velocity its shape (hold, log, linear, exp, S), Length its position. In LIVE, Value records the gesture.
Fills: while playing, hold PLAY = fill as long as held; tap PLAY = fill on the next bar; STOP stops. Each step plays always, only in a fill, or never in a fill (Length ▸ push, or the Fill display mode). ⇧ in the Master context: auto fill every N loops.
Tracks: one page per instrument. ⇧ + white key N opens track N (latch SHIFT, or hold Shift, then click the key).
Device keyboard: without REC it plays the track. REC (or Enter): in PATTERN the white keys are the steps, in ROLL each key writes its note and advances. REC + PLAY (⇧REC, or Enter + Space): live recording.
